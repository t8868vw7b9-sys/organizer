🎒 RS3 Bank Layout Guide (Alt1)

Since Alt1 cannot move items for you, use this overlay checklist pane to sort your tabs side-by-side with your open bank.

Ready to assist. Open your bank in-game.

🏠 Tab 0: General & Currencies

Your drop-everything-here dump zone.

  • Coins, Spirit Shards, and Bonds
  • Teleport items (Max Guild, War's Retreat)
  • Current active slayer/boss drops

⚔️ Tab 1: PVM & Combat Gear

Keep your absolute best gear ready for rapid deployment.

  • T90+ Power Armour sets (Melee, Range, Mage, Necro)
  • BiS Weapons, EOF Amulets, and God Books
  • Vulnerability bombs, Powerbursts, Adrenaline renewals

🧪 Tab 2: Herblore & Potions

Order by dose size to prevent bank slot wastage.

  • Clean/Grimy Herbs & Secondary ingredients
  • Overloads, Extremes, and Super sets
  • Flasks arranged dynamically (6-dose down to 1-dose)

🌲 Tab 3: Skilling & Resources

Group materials together based on current leveling goals.

  • Logs, Ores, Bars, and Uncut Gems
  • Archeology materials and damaged artifacts
  • Urns, Proteans, and Skilling Outfits
